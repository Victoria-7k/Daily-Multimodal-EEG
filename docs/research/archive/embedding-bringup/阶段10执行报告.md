### 10.5 服务器全量对照

- 状态：完成
- 服务器命令：
  - `python scripts/10_run_upgrade_ablation.py --embeddings outputs/embeddings/all_complete_basic_embeddings.npz --baseline outputs/reports/baseline_reference_metrics.json --upgrade modality_token_attention --target-label alert --out-table outputs/reports/model_upgrade_ablation_table.md --failures-out outputs/reports/model_upgrade_failures.json --metrics-out outputs/reports/modality_token_fusion_metrics.json --model-out outputs/models/modality_token_fusion.pt --epochs 120 --overfit-limit 128`
- ablation 结果：
  - baseline full test RMSE：`0.8756`

  - test：`count=217`，`MAE=0.6117`，`RMSE=0.6968`，`Pearson=0.1950`
- overfit check：
  - `sample_count=128`
  - `initial_loss=0.7904`
  - `final_loss=0.5081`
  - `passed=true`
- 失败清单：
  - `outputs/reports/model_upgrade_failures.json` 为 `[]`
- 当前结论：阶段 10 第一轮融合升级验收通过；`modality_token_attention` 可作为当前融合层进阶结果保留，同时 baseline reference 仍作为后续模态升级的回退基准。
